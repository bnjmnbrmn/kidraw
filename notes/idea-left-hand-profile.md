# Left-hand-dominant profile

A profile where the left hand drives movement (e.g. `f d s e` for ←↓↑→) and the right hand triggers submenus. Mirror of the right-hand-dominant `ijkl` layout.

Rationale: many users prefer the mouse in the right hand for non-kidraw apps, and even though kidraw is keyboard-first, having the keyboard "home position" leave the right hand free could ease context-switching. Also genuinely useful for left-handed users using a mouse on the right side of the body.

Blocked on: deciding the right-hand submenu trigger keys, and confirming whether the keymenu visualization needs to be horizontally mirrored or whether the existing layout still reads well. Not on the critical path.
