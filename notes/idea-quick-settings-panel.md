# Quick settings panel (orthogonal to keymenu modes)

Several settings act like "modes" but holding a key to enter them is awkward — e.g. "move by node" or "graph move." A persistent settings panel (sidebar or similar) might be preferable for mode-like state that should *stick*: movement mode, edge directedness default, node shape default, edge color, line style, font. The temporary mode-switch-via-hold (e.g. `t` / `g`) can stay alongside.

The alternative — more roots and submenus — runs into exponential menu explosion. A flat settings panel is orthogonal to the keymenu, not a submenu.
