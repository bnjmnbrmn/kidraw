Question three

How does it work?

Question threeHow does it work?

Tapping a key and holding it are different commands.

Inside a submenu, h j k l can mean something else.

The lift mark in the corner of a key card means it fires when the key comes back up.

Insert and normal, w and b, x and dd.

Question four

Important features?

Question fourImportant features?

Computed, not drawn, and computed again whenever anything moves.

A point the route has to pass through, for when the automatic one is wrong.

On a keypress, not on every edit.

Two modifiers change how far one press moves the crosshairs.

Shape, colour, and solid, dotted or dashed.

Text on an arrow rather than in a box.

To your own disk. No account, no server.

It uses the file system access API. A fallback for other browsers is not written yet.

Push a box into an arrow, and the arrow goes around it

An arrow should not disappear behind a box. Plan points at Ship it, with Review below them; as Ship it is dragged down, the straight line would run through Review, so the route bends over it instead — recomputed on every keypress.

Three boxes. Plan points at Ship it; Review sits below, out of the way.1 / 6

The small circle on the line is the point the route is pulled through; dropping one by hand makes it a waypoint you control.

Before you open it

It is an alpha. Bring a keyboard.

Chrome, a physical keyboard, and some patience. Saving is local-only and Chrome-only, and some arrows still route worse than you would draw them.
