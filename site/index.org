* KiDraw
** What is it?
*** Diagram editor
*** Keyboard-oriented
- Inspired by the vi coding editor
- Explorable keybindings
*** WIP experiment

** What's the point?
*** Efficiency
**** Writing _and_ Connecting
- Get both text and connections down, before they slip away
**** Navigation
- Skip the empty space
- Follow the links
**** Learning
- Explore the keybindings, without reading the manual

** How does it work?
*** Press and hold submenu keys
*** Some submenu keys allow you to change how you navigate
*** Some submenu keys have an action on release
*** Press ESC or Ctrl-[ once or twice to get back to the main menu
*** Vi-style modal text editing

** What are some important features?
*** Automatic edge routing
**** Waypoints for fine tuning
*** Node/edge layout on demand
*** Coarse and fine movement
*** Navigate by hopping from node to node
*** Navigate by following edges
*** Zoom, pan, and recenter
*** Node and Edge Styling
*** Edge labels
*** Saving diagrams locally
**** currently Chrome only
